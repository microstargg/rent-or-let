# Let Flow Customer Terms

**DRAFT for solicitor review. Not legal advice.**

These terms (the "Agreement") are between **[Let Flow legal entity]**, company number [number],
registered office [address] ("Let Flow", "we"), and the letting agency named on the Order Form
(the "Customer", "you"). They apply from the date you sign the Order Form or first use the
Services, whichever is earlier.

## 1. The Services

1.1 Let Flow provides property management software for letting agencies, including the office
system, tenant and landlord portals, a public agency website, listing feeds and related support
(the "Services"), as described on the Order Form.

1.2 We will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care. We aim for the Services to be
available [99.5]% of each month, excluding planned maintenance we tell you about in advance and
events outside our reasonable control.

1.3 We may improve and change the Services. We will not make a change that materially reduces
the core functions you pay for without giving you at least 30 days' notice and the right to end
the Agreement.

## 2. Your account and users

2.1 You decide who in your business gets staff access and with what role, and you invite
landlords, tenants and contractors to the portals.

2.2 You are responsible for your users' actions, for keeping log-in details secure, and for
removing access when someone leaves.

2.3 You must not use the Services to break the law, to send unsolicited marketing, to upload
malicious code, or to try to access other customers' data.

## 3. Your data

3.1 You own the data you and your users put into the Services ("Customer Data").

3.2 You give us the right to host, copy, process and display Customer Data only as needed to
provide, secure and support the Services.

3.3 You are the controller of personal data in Customer Data and we are your processor. The
[Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md) forms part of this Agreement and
governs how we handle it.

3.4 You are responsible for: having a lawful basis for the personal data you enter; giving your
applicants, tenants, landlords and staff a privacy notice (we provide a template on your agency
website, which you must check and keep accurate); paying the ICO data protection fee; deciding
how long to keep data; and meeting your other legal duties as a letting agent, including Right
to Rent, anti-money laundering, deposit protection, client money protection and redress scheme
membership.

3.5 You can export your Customer Data at any time while the Agreement is in force. After it
ends we will return and delete Customer Data as set out in the DPA.

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 You will pay the fees on the Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, fees are
billed monthly in advance, are exclusive of VAT and are due within 14 days of invoice.

4.2 We may increase fees once a year by giving you at least 60 days' notice. If you do not
accept the increase you may end the Agreement before it takes effect.

4.3 If an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days overdue, we may suspend the Services after
giving you 14 days' written notice. We will not delete Customer Data while the Services are
suspended.

## 5. Term and ending the Agreement

5.1 The Agreement runs for the initial term on the Order Form and then renews monthly until
either party gives 30 days' written notice.

5.2 Either party may end the Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a
material breach that it does not fix within 30 days of being asked, or becomes insolvent.

5.3 When the Agreement ends, your right to use the Services stops, and clause 3.5 and the DPA
apply to Customer Data.

## 6. Intellectual property

6.1 We own the Services, the software and everything we create to provide them. You get a
non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Services for your agency during the Agreement.

6.2 Your brand, content and Customer Data remain yours. You give us permission to use your
name and logo to run your agency website and, with your written consent, to name you as a
customer.

## 7. AI features

7.1 Some features use artificial intelligence to draft listing descriptions and replies. AI
output may be wrong, and your staff must review it before it is used or sent.

7.2 We use AI providers only on terms that stop them using Customer Data to train their models.
They are listed as sub-processors in the DPA.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for this
Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by
negligence, fraud, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.

9.2 Neither party is liable for loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings, or
for indirect or consequential loss.

9.3 Subject to 9.1, each party's total liability in each year of the Agreement is limited to
the fees paid and payable in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.
*[Solicitor to advise on a separate, higher cap for data protection breaches and confidentiality,
and on insurance cover.]*

9.4 You are responsible for decisions you make using the Services, such as letting decisions,
rent arrears action and legal notices. The Services help you manage your obligations but do not
replace professional or legal advice.

## 10. General

10.1 Neither party may transfer this Agreement without the other's consent, except we may
transfer it to a buyer of our business on notice to you.

10.2 Notices must be in writing and sent by email to the addresses on the Order Form.

10.3 If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest continues.

10.4 This Agreement, the Order Form and the DPA are the whole agreement between us. If they
conflict, the DPA wins for personal data, then the Order Form, then these terms.

10.5 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and
Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
