## Who we are

[Let Flow legal entity] ("Let Flow", "we") makes property management software for UK letting
agencies. Company number [number], registered office [address]. We are registered with the
Information Commissioner's Office under number [ICO registration number].

Contact us about privacy at [privacy@letflow.app].

## When we are the controller, and when we are not

This notice covers personal data we decide how to use:

- people who visit letflow.app or ask for a demo;
- staff at our customer agencies, for running their account, billing and support.

When an agency uses Let Flow to manage its tenants, landlords, applicants and contractors, the
**agency** is the controller of that data and we process it for them under a data processing
agreement. If you rent from or let through an agency that uses Let Flow, please read that
agency's privacy notice and contact the agency about your data.

## What we collect

- **Demo and contact forms:** name, work email, phone number if given, agency name, portfolio
  size and your message.
- **Customer accounts:** names, work emails, roles and log-in records of agency staff; billing
  contacts and payment records.
- **Support:** what you tell us when you ask for help.
- **Website use:** anonymous page view statistics from Vercel Analytics, which does not use
  cookies or identify you, and server logs (including IP address) kept by our host for
  security.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Replying to demo requests and enquiries | Legitimate interests in responding to businesses interested in Let Flow |
| Providing the service, support and billing to customers | Contract |
| Sending service updates and security notices to customer staff | Contract; legitimate interests |
| Occasional product news to business contacts, with an easy opt-out | Legitimate interests (PECR allows this for corporate subscribers) |
| Keeping the site and service secure | Legitimate interests |
| Accounting and tax records | Legal obligation |

We do not sell personal data.

## Who we share it with

Hosting (Vercel), database (Neon), email (Resend), payments (Stripe) and our professional
advisers. Some of these providers are in the United States; we rely on the UK Extension to the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement.

## How long we keep it

- Demo requests that do not become customers: 2 years from last contact
- Customer account and billing records: 6 years after the contract ends
- Server logs: as set by our host, normally under 30 days

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it,
or ask us to stop sending marketing. Email [privacy@letflow.app]. We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

## Cookies

letflow.app does not set tracking or advertising cookies, so we do not show a cookie banner. If
that changes, we will ask for your consent first.
